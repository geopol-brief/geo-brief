  ## What happened

  Four threads ran through the week. First, on 19 November Trump, Xi and Putin held their first three-way meeting in Shenzhen on the APEC sidelines. Reports describe a four-point declaration covering UN reform, oil supply, state immunity and a dialogue on nuclear risks. No date was set for nuclear talks, and the text did not mention Ukraine. Sources disagree on trade: Fox News relays a claim of tariff cuts on about $30 billion of trade, EconoTimes says China's Commerce Ministry described a framework still subject to domestic procedures, and IBTimes and CFR report no new trade deal.

  Second, the Commerce Department's BIS issued on 16 November an interim final rule on remote access to advanced chips, adding licence requirements for Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand and Brazil. Third, Brent stayed near $92-93 (about $92.01 on 19 November; a $92.57 settle reported on 20 November by a secondary aggregator). Iran keeps reporting mine strikes that no maritime authority has confirmed; a secondary report says UKMTO logged two tankers hit by projectiles. Flow data conflict: IMF PortWatch counted 2 transits on 15 November against a pre-crisis 85 a day, while Oilprice describes exports continuing through ship-to-ship relays.

  Fourth, the EU Council adopted on 16 November the roughly 145 Russia-related listings (Steptoe), and on 19 November the Berlin Coalition of the Willing met. Moscow still has not accepted a European seat at the table, and the Commission is preparing a 22nd sanctions package.

  ## Why it matters

  The week separated signal from substance. Shenzhen produced a photograph and principles, not measures, so the 10 January 2027 expiry of the US-China suspensions remains the operative date. The chip rule shows Washington tightening controls in parallel with summitry. In the Gulf, price stability rests on supply-glut expectations rather than on confirmed safe passage, which makes the market sensitive to any verified incident. In Europe, negotiation and pressure advance together.

  ## Implications for companies

  ### Supply chain
  Keep the rare-earth magnet and graphite exposure list tied to 10 January 2027; sources contradict each other on whether Beijing is easing or throttling heavy rare-earth supply, so verify with suppliers directly. Map sulfur, aluminium and petrochemical inputs that transit Hormuz.

  ### Regulation
  Check whether Southeast Asian data-centre or server counterparties host China-headquartered customers under the BIS rule. Screen counterparties against the 16 November EU listings; one report says UAE, Hong Kong, Seychelles and Oman firms were named, which other sources did not confirm.

  ### Market
  Do not budget the reported $30 billion tariff cut until a Chinese Commerce Ministry or tariff-commission notice gives HS codes and dates. Test freight, insurance and energy-cost assumptions at both a calm and a disrupted Gulf scenario.

  ## What to watch next

  - 26-27 November: European Council; the Commission is reported to present the 22nd Russia package and Ukraine's 2027 financing (Steptoe)
  - 30 November: second CBAM downstream trilogue (press report, carried over from last week)
  - 9 December: provisional resumption of EU Critical Raw Materials Act talks
  - 14-15 December: G20 summit at Miami's Doral resort; Xi is expected, Putin's attendance unclear (CFR)
  - Verification by UKMTO or insurers of Iran's mine claims; Federal Register text and effective date of the BIS rule
  - 10 January 2027: expiry of the US-China suspensions

## 무슨 일이 있었나

이번 주에는 네 흐름이 이어졌습니다. 첫째, 11월 19일 선전 APEC 계기에 트럼프, 시진핑, 푸틴이 첫 3자 회동을 가졌습니다. 보도에 따르면 유엔 개혁, 석유 공급, 국가 면책, 핵 위험 감축 대화를 담은 4개 항 선언이 나왔으나 핵 협상 일정은 정해지지 않았고 우크라이나는 언급되지 않았습니다. 무역은 보도가 엇갈립니다. 폭스뉴스는 약 300억 달러 규모 관세 인하 주장을 전했고, 에코타임스는 중국 상무부가 국내 절차를 전제로 한 틀이라고 설명했다고 보도했으며, IBTimes와 CFR은 새 무역 합의가 없었다고 봤습니다.

둘째, 미 상무부 BIS가 11월 16일 첨단 칩 원격접속 잠정최종규칙을 내고 인도네시아·말레이시아·싱가포르·태국·브라질행 허가 요건을 추가했습니다. 셋째, 브렌트유는 92~93달러대에 머물렀습니다(19일 92.01달러, 20일 종가 92.57달러는 2차 집계 사이트 보도). 이란은 기뢰 피격을 계속 주장하지만 해사 당국의 확인은 없고, 2차 보도는 UKMTO가 유조선 2척의 발사체 피격을 기록했다고 전합니다. 통항 자료도 엇갈려, IMF 포트워치는 11월 15일 통항 2건(개전 전 일 85건)을 집계한 반면 오일프라이스는 선박 간 환적으로 수출이 이어진다고 전합니다.

넷째, EU 이사회는 11월 16일 약 145건의 러시아 관련 제재 명단을 채택했고(Steptoe), 19일 베를린에서 의지의 연합 회의가 열렸습니다. 러시아는 유럽의 협상 참여를 아직 받아들이지 않았고, 집행위는 제22차 제재 패키지를 준비 중입니다.
