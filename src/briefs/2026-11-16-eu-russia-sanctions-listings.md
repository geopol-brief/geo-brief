  ## What happened

  On Wednesday 11 November, EU ambassadors (Coreper) in Brussels agreed a new set of sanctions listings against Russia. Reuters, as carried by Global Banking & Finance, counted 145 listings: 113 under the main Ukraine regime (including 50 Russian banks, shadow-fleet enablers, energy companies and business figures), 31 under the hybrid-threats regime and one under the cyber regime. TASS, citing a Brussels source, reported 43 individuals and about 100 legal entities. The two counts differ and the Council had not published the legal acts at the time of writing.

  Foreign ministers are expected to adopt the listings at their Monday 16 November meeting in Brussels, with entry into force on publication in the Official Journal. TASS's source said the listings are separate from the 22nd package, which is still being negotiated, and unrelated to the US-Russia-Ukraine talks held in Abu Dhabi on 10-11 November. Reports that third-country firms in the UAE, Hong Kong, Oman and the Seychelles are included are unconfirmed.

  The G7 foreign ministers, meeting in Paris on 9-10 November, had already pledged to tighten sanctions on shadow-fleet vessels, false-flag ships, insurers and oil traders, and condemned Iran's obstruction of transit through the Strait of Hormuz.

  ## Why it matters

  The listings continue a pattern of widening from Russian entities to the intermediaries around them. The 21st package, adopted on 24 July, took the number of listed vessels to 673, created a framework for transaction bans on refineries in Russia and third countries that process Russian crude, and suspended the automatic adjustment of the G7 oil price cap until 15 July 2027, citing market disruption from Hormuz. The new listings add to that body of measures at a time when the diplomatic track is moving without EU capitals.

  ## Implications for companies

  ### Supply chain
  Screen freight forwarders, charterers, bunkering and ship-management counterparties against the new names once the legal acts are published, and ask carriers to confirm in writing that the vessels carrying your cargo are not listed. For manufacturers, the exposure is mostly indirect: energy, petrochemical-derived inputs and ocean freight pass through the same intermediaries.

  ### Regulation
  Review whether sanctions clauses in supply, charter and insurance contracts cover newly listed parties and allow suspension without penalty. Banks and trade-finance providers may reject payments touching listed institutions, so check payment routes for third-country banks.

  ### Market
  Expect tighter insurance and financing terms for cargo that touches the Gulf or Russian energy trade, because the G7 statement signals pressure on insurers and traders as well as vessels. Treat any price effect as a possibility, not a forecast.

  ## What to watch next

  - 16 November: formal adoption by foreign ministers and publication in the Official Journal, including the final counts
  - Whether the listings name third-country entities, which would be the main change for non-EU suppliers
  - Progress on the 22nd package and on the US-led ceasefire talks, including the proposed halt to strikes on energy infrastructure and Black Sea shipping

## 왜 중요한가

이번 조치는 러시아 법인에서 주변 중개 업체로 제재 범위를 넓혀 온 흐름의 연장입니다. 7월 24일 채택된 제21차 패키지는 제재 대상 선박을 673척으로 늘렸고, 러시아산 원유를 처리하는 러시아·제3국 정유소와의 거래를 금지할 수 있는 틀을 만들었으며, G7 유가상한제의 자동 조정을 호르무즈 사태에 따른 시장 혼란을 이유로 2027년 7월 15일까지 중단했습니다. 이번 명단은 EU 회원국이 배제된 외교 협상이 진행되는 시점에 그 위에 얹히는 조치입니다.

## 기업 시사점

### 공급망
관보에 법적 문서가 게재되면 포워더, 용선주, 벙커링, 선박관리 거래 상대방을 신규 명단과 대조하고, 운송사에 화물을 싣는 선박이 제재 대상이 아님을 서면으로 확인받으십시오. 제조업체의 노출은 대체로 간접적이며, 에너지, 석유화학 파생 투입재, 해상 운임이 같은 중개망을 거칩니다.

### 규제
공급·용선·보험 계약의 제재 조항이 신규 지정 당사자를 포함하는지, 위약금 없이 이행을 중단할 수 있는지 검토합니다. 은행과 무역금융 기관이 지정 금융기관을 거치는 결제를 거절할 수 있으므로 제3국 은행을 경유하는 결제 경로를 점검합니다.

### 시장
G7 성명이 선박뿐 아니라 보험사와 트레이더에 대한 압박을 예고했으므로, 걸프 지역이나 러시아 에너지 거래와 닿는 화물은 보험·금융 조건이 까다로워질 수 있습니다. 가격 영향은 예측이 아니라 가능성으로만 보아야 합니다.
