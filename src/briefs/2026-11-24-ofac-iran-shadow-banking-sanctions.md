  ## What happened

  On Monday 23 November 2026, the US Treasury's Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC) announced new Iran-related designations aimed at the country's shadow banking network. Press reports put the total at "more than two dozen" exchange houses, front companies and individuals, with operators in Iran, Dubai, Hong Kong and Turkey. VOZ reported that one designation was made under Executive Order 13902 (the Iran Center for Exchange of Currency and Gold) and another under counterterrorism authority, Executive Order 13224 (H N B Trading Co LLC). Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent described the system as "the last financial lifeline of a bankrupt regime". The action continues "Operation Economic Outcast", which Treasury launched on 24 August 2026. We could not retrieve the 23 November Treasury release, so the counts and legal authorities above rest on press reports and are not confirmed against the primary text.

  ## Why it matters

  The designations target the payment channel rather than oil cargoes themselves: exchange houses and trading fronts that move money for Iranian trade. The action coincides with military pressure. Gulf News reported on 23 November that President Trump threatened to sink Iranian vessels trying to breach the US naval blockade, that a reported US strike on a tanker carrying Iranian oil was unconfirmed by either government, and that Pakistan and Qatar were still mediating. Financial and naval pressure are therefore running in parallel while talks continue.

  ## Implications for companies

  ### Supply chain
  Third-country trading intermediaries in Dubai, Hong Kong and Turkey are the exposed link. Companies that buy energy, petrochemicals, metals or battery-related materials through traders in those hubs should screen each trading counterparty and its owners against the updated SDN list and the 50 percent ownership rule.

  ### Regulation
  Foreign banks that knowingly process significant transactions for designated persons risk losing US correspondent accounts, according to the reports. Confirm that sanctions clauses, termination rights and payment-source warranties in supplier and distributor contracts cover newly designated parties.

  ### Market
  Expect banks in the Gulf and Hong Kong to tighten onboarding and payment checks, which can lengthen settlement times for small traders. Review payment terms and buffer periods with suppliers that rely on such intermediaries.

  ## What to watch next

  - The text of the 23 November OFAC release and updated SDN list entries, to confirm the number of designations and authorities.
  - Further steps under Operation Economic Outcast, including any action against foreign banks.
  - Whether the Pakistan- and Qatar-mediated talks continue after the blockade threats.
  - Incident reports from UKMTO for the Gulf of Oman and Hormuz, which affect tanker insurance and freight rates.

## 왜 중요한가

이번 제재는 원유 선적 자체보다 이란 무역의 자금을 옮기는 환전소와 무역 위장회사, 즉 결제 경로를 겨냥합니다. 군사적 압박도 병행되고 있습니다. 걸프뉴스는 11월 23일 트럼프 대통령이 미국의 해상 봉쇄를 뚫으려는 이란 선박을 침몰시키겠다고 위협했고, 이란산 원유를 실은 유조선을 미국이 타격했다는 보도는 양국 모두 확인하지 않았으며, 파키스탄과 카타르의 중재가 이어지고 있다고 전했습니다. 금융 압박과 해상 압박이 협상과 동시에 진행되는 구도입니다.

## 기업 시사점

### 공급망
노출이 큰 곳은 두바이, 홍콩, 튀르키예의 제3국 중개 무역업체입니다. 이 지역 트레이더를 통해 에너지, 석유화학, 금속, 배터리 관련 소재를 조달하는 기업은 거래 상대방과 그 소유 구조를 갱신된 SDN 목록과 50% 소유 규칙에 따라 대조해야 합니다.

### 규제
보도에 따르면 지정 대상자와의 중요한 거래를 알면서 처리한 외국 은행은 미국 내 환거래 계좌를 잃을 수 있습니다. 공급·유통 계약의 제재 조항, 해지권, 대금 출처 보증이 새로 지정된 대상까지 포괄하는지 확인해야 합니다.

### 시장
걸프와 홍콩의 은행이 신규 거래 심사와 결제 점검을 강화하면 소규모 트레이더의 정산이 지연될 수 있습니다. 이런 중개업체에 의존하는 공급사와는 결제 조건과 여유 기간을 재검토하는 것이 좋습니다.
