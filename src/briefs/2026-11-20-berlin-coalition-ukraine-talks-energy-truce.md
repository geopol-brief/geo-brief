  ## What happened

  On Thursday 19 November, Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky met European leaders and the US envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner in Berlin at a Coalition of the Willing meeting. In a joint statement afterwards, the leaders of Germany, France, the UK and Ukraine called on Russia to agree an energy ceasefire for the duration of the winter and a ceasefire allowing Black Sea food exports. They also pledged more air defence and financial support, including 90 billion euros under the EU's Ukraine Support Loan, and said they would raise economic pressure, with more sanctions on Russian oil and cryptocurrency and action against circumvention.

  The meeting followed the first US-brokered Russia-Ukraine talks in nine months, held in Abu Dhabi around 10 November, which reportedly produced a prisoner-exchange agreement but no breakthrough. On 16 November Kyiv, Washington and five European governments held online talks. Kremlin aide Yuri Ushakov said Russia had not agreed to European participation in the next round. After Berlin, Zelensky said Ukraine wants the US to help organise a first team-level multilateral meeting with Europe fully present "as soon as possible"; no date has been set.

  ## Why it matters

  Two tracks now run side by side. Washington is pushing a negotiated outline, while Europe is trying to secure a seat at the table and keep up pressure through sanctions and financing. Moscow, according to Reuters, still demands that Ukraine cede all of Donbas, and the first meeting that includes Europe has no date. Strikes on energy infrastructure have continued: Al Jazeera reported on 17 November one of the largest overnight Ukrainian drone attacks on Russia, including refineries, alongside Russian strikes on Ukraine.

  ## Implications for companies

  ### Supply chain
  List the inputs and logistics legs with Russian, Black Sea or Baltic exposure, including energy-intensive materials and freight routed through the region. Check how an energy-infrastructure truce, or its absence, would change winter power and fuel costs at your European plants.

  ### Regulation
  Further sanctions on Russian oil and crypto are signalled but not yet adopted. Review counterparty screening and anti-circumvention clauses now, and track the next EU package for the dates and the entities it names.

  ### Market
  Energy prices are exposed to both outcomes: a truce would ease refinery and grid risk, while a breakdown of talks would keep it. For battery and EV producers, test cell-plant energy cost and freight assumptions against both scenarios, without assuming a settlement.

  ## What to watch next

  - Whether a date is set for the first multilateral meeting with European delegates, and whether Russia accepts their participation
  - Russia's response to the winter energy truce proposal and any change in strikes on energy infrastructure
  - The next EU sanctions package targeting Russian oil and crypto, and disbursement steps under the 90 billion euro loan

협상과 압박이 나란히 움직이고 있습니다. 미국은 협상 골격을 밀고, 유럽은 협상 테이블의 자리와 제재·재정 지원을 통한 압박을 동시에 확보하려 합니다. 로이터에 따르면 러시아는 여전히 돈바스 전체의 양도를 요구하고 있고, 유럽이 참여하는 첫 회의는 날짜가 없습니다. 에너지 시설에 대한 타격도 이어집니다. 알자지라는 11월 17일 정유시설을 포함한 우크라이나의 대규모 야간 드론 공격과 러시아의 공습이 병행되고 있다고 보도했습니다.
